# Using Auto ExecSec

## Prepare and approve reminders

1. **People:** enter First name, Last name and Phone in editable rows, then **Save people** and confirm. Use **Add person** or the row's delete control; use Edit beside a saved person to correct their existing identity. Local numbers default to US unless you configured another country in Setup; explicit `+` codes take precedence. Numbers are normalized to E.164 using libphonenumber; that is format validation, not ownership or SMS-capability verification.
2. **Schedule:** enter Date, Time and Name in editable appointment rows. Calendar, clock and saved-person buttons avoid unnecessary typing. **Add group member** prepares one shared MMS, not separate texts. Each new row captures the phone zone; check the visible zone and change it explicitly when needed.
3. **Import, optionally:** the **Import** button beside each table opens file selection or CSV paste. [Supported CSV and legacy formats](formats.md#csv-imports) load the same editable rows. Invalid imports leave your table unchanged; replacing nonempty rows requires **Replace rows**. Imported People are not saved until you confirm Save people. Imports are foreground-only; source text, unrelated fields and selected URI are not stored. No clipboard scraping or durable URI permission.
4. **Review messages:** resolve every recipient, inspect every exact phone number, rendered message, appointment/send times, zone, SIM and transport. Individuals show SMS segments; groups show shared MMS and a number/reply visibility warning. Change appointment fields and press **Update preview** before approval, or **Back to table** to edit the rows. Invalid/ambiguous rows block approval. Entry and imports alone never send.
5. **Approve:** check the authorization box and confirm the reminders. Every member, message and SIM subscription is frozen. The default lead time is 120 minutes. At most 40 reminders per approval, 6 segments per individual SMS, and 20 distinct members per group (or the carrier's smaller limit).
6. **Queue:** inspect pending/submitting/sent/failed/uncertain/expired/cancelled outcomes. Refresh while the app is open to fetch new callbacks and capability status. Pending reminders can be cancelled or edited; editing cancels the prior approval and opens a new review. Changes to a recipient, template or lead time invalidate affected pending approvals. Reapproval is explicit.

Approved reminders are not silently redirected if the selected SIM disappears. There is a 40-active-reminder cap and a conservative local throttle of 40 submission attempts per hour; carrier/platform multipart rate limits can still reject messages. Submitted SMS cannot be edited, cancelled or recalled. Carrier SMS charges may apply; SMS is not RCS.

Table and Announcement composition drafts remain in memory across navigation and Activity recreation, not across process death. Save People before closing the app; review and approve schedules deliberately. Draft text is not copied into unencrypted instance-state bundles. Secondary formats, settings and explanations are expandable; capability blockers, exact previews, group visibility warnings and approval controls remain explicit.

## Copy-only text templates

Open **Templates** using the document icon in the top app bar. Create a named template such as `Hello {name}, your meeting is at {time}.`, save it, then fill the generated fields and **Copy output** to paste into another app. Each distinct case-sensitive variable has one labeled field; repeating `{name}` reuses its value everywhere. Definitions with no variables copy their literal text. Edit/delete controls act only on these definitions, not appointment or Announcement templates; this feature never approves or sends messages.

Variable labels start with an ASCII letter, followed by letters, digits, spaces, underscores or hyphens (up to 64 characters, no surrounding spaces). Missing/nested/unmatched braces are errors; braces are reserved for variables. Up to 32 distinct fields, 50 definitions, 16,000 template characters and 64,000 output characters are supported. Every field must be filled before copying. Values are substituted once: braces inside an entered value are ordinary text, not another template.

Definitions use the existing encrypted local database, backup exclusions and viewing lock, with no new permissions or schema migration. Full local-data deletion removes them; completed-history deletion does not. Filled values and unsaved definition edits stay only in screen memory and are discarded on navigation or process/configuration recreation. Copy is explicit and marks clipboard content sensitive on supported Android versions; this flag hides supported clipboard previews, not all clipboard access. Other apps and device clipboard features may retain pasted/copied text.

## Announcements

**Announcements** is separate from appointment schedules and shared group MMS. Compose once, select saved People, and review every exact rendered message, unique normalized number, selected SIM, per-recipient/total SMS segments, delay and queue expiry. **People & import** opens the same editable People table without losing your composition. Up to **100 unique numbers** and **6 SMS segments per number** are supported. These are app limits, not carrier allowances. Aliases with the same E.164 number are deduplicated; review the retained person's name and text. Different numbers never merge because names match. Save/import at most 40 People rows at once; use additional saves for a larger list.

**Reusable templates:** expand **Templates & pacing** to save/load a local Announcement template and delay, independently of appointment templates. You can also copy the existing individual appointment template without changing it. The same template renderer supports `{FirstName}` (also `{firstName}` / `{Firstname}`) and `{LastName}`, rendered separately for each recipient. `{LastName}` requires their explicit saved surname. Announcements have no appointment context: `{time}` / `{dayPeriod}` cannot be rendered here; use appointment reminders for appointment fields. Unknown tokens, unmatched braces, missing fields and unresolved placeholders block preview/approval instead of being sent. Each rendered message is independently sized and frozen.

**Configurable pacing:** enter a whole-number delay of **1-3,600 seconds**, default **5 seconds**. These are configuration bounds, not safe carrier rates. The delay is a minimum wait after the preceding recipient's successful submission results (all SMS parts), not a precise send schedule and not a delivery confirmation. Approval freezes the delay; saving another preference does not change an active batch. Pacing state survives restart and history deletion while recipients remain queued. The preview shows the configured wait total; long waits, Android scheduling, shared throttling and missing capabilities can leave queued recipients expired. **Pacing does not guarantee delivery or carrier permission.**

The flow prominently displays consent, terms-of-use, carrier, Android, charges and privacy warnings. **Both affirmative acknowledgments and the final confirmation are required for every batch.** Existing contacts/saved People are not evidence of consent. Use only for occasional, expected messages that recipients consented to receive; exclude opt-outs and honor applicable law. There is no automatic opt-out ingestion: replies remain in the messaging app, so check them and stop remaining sends/remove opted-out recipients yourself.

**Check your actual carrier and plan terms before use. Consent and unlimited texting do not establish that automated/bulk messaging is permitted.** Carriers may filter or block messages or restrict/suspend service. No count or rate is guaranteed safe, and delivery is not guaranteed. Android may impose limits or ask for confirmations. Normal SMS/multipart charges can apply. These disclaimers neither grant carrier permission nor waive legal duties. Google Play permission policy is separate from Android's technical permissions; no Play approval is claimed.

Approval freezes each individual recipient, rendered message, segment count, delay and SIM in encrypted local storage. Batches share the existing **40 submission attempts/hour** safeguard with appointments; a 100-number batch takes over two hours or longer, and competes for that shared allowance. Due appointments are considered before batch rows. Queued batch messages expire **24 hours after preview**, not after an appointment grace period. Review authorization expires after 15 minutes. Only one batch can be active, and only one of its recipients can await submission results at a time. Any failed, expired or uncertain submission stops the remaining queued batch; there are no automatic retries. Process recovery preserves pending approvals and classifies an interrupted submission as uncertain rather than resending it. Editing/deleting a selected person cancels their pending send; later appointment or batch template/delay preference changes never rewrite approved batch payloads.

Open Announcements for automatically refreshed individual progress, accepted/failed/uncertain/cancelled outcomes and reported delivery. **Stop remaining queued sends** cannot recall submitted SMS. Manually review uncertain outcomes in the normal messaging app; acknowledgment records review, not delivery. History has no resend action. A completed/stopped approval cannot be approved again, including after history deletion; a genuinely new announcement requires a new preview and acknowledgments. Deleting app history does not delete the phone's messaging history or provider records.

## Shared group MMS

Shared reminders require carrier support for group MMS and may require mobile data. Check your plan for any MMS charges.

Everyone's numbers and replies are visible in a shared conversation. Outgoing history and replies belong in the normal default Messages app; Auto ExecSec does not read them. A successful MMS sent callback means carrier send success, **not** per-person delivery or verified reply-to-all/thread behavior. Group history reports delivery as unavailable. Missing callbacks or process interruption can leave an uncertain outcome; the app does not retry it.

Android or the carrier may finish sending after the appointment. Submitted MMS cannot be recalled. The app never resubmits an unknown outcome or substitutes separate SMS. See [validation evidence](validation.md) for the limited Samsung group MMS result.

## Night-before schedule-input notification

Default: Monday-Friday **reminder-work days**, notified on the preceding evening at **20:00 in the phone's local time zone**. For Monday, the prompt is on Sunday evening. Additional one-off work dates and a configurable local evening time are supported.

Prompts are independent of imported appointments: the app cannot know an unentered schedule. A date with an approved schedule, or explicitly marked **no reminders**, suppresses its preceding-evening prompt. Marking no reminders suppresses the prompt; it does not cancel already approved reminders. The prompt opens Import and contains no names, times, numbers or message bodies.
